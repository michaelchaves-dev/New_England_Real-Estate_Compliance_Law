# New England Landlord-Tenant Compliance Registry

A central registry covering state tenant and landlord law, statutory frameworks, and regulatory compliance across New England jurisdictions (**MA · NH · ME · VT · CT**), including N.E. renters’ rights by state.

> **Disclaimer:** This repository is for informational and organizational purposes only. It is not legal advice. Always verify statutes, regulations, and case law against official sources.

---

## Core Concepts & Legal Frameworks

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Landlord-Tenant Law** | The specific legal framework covering leases, rent, and property rules. |
| **Tenancy** | The status, period, or condition of holding land or property as a tenant. |
| **Real Property Law** | The broader branch of civil law covering all real estate and property rights. |
| **Housing Code** | Local or state rules setting minimum safety and health standards for rental homes. |
| **Habitability** | The legal standard requiring landlords to provide safe, livable housing. |
| **Leasehold** | The legal interest or estate a tenant holds in a property. |
| **Property Code** | The official title of state law volumes governing landlord-tenant relationships. |
| **Conveyancing** | The broader legal process of transferring property interests from one party to another. |
| **Jurisdictional** | Relating to the territory or bounds within which legal authority is exercised. |

## Compliance Categories

### Regulatory & Legal

- **Statutory Compliance** — Adhering to specific state property codes, including security deposit limits and eviction timelines.
- **Regulatory Compliance** — Following rules set by administrative agencies such as HUD, OSHA, or state housing authorities.
- **Habitability Compliance** — Meeting state and local building, health, and safety codes to maintain livable properties.
- **Fair Housing Compliance** — Adhering to anti-discrimination laws under the federal Fair Housing Act (FHA) and state equivalents.

---

## Operational Roles

- **Landlord Compliance** — Operational rules landlords must execute, such as mandatory disclosures and lead paint notices.
- **Tenant Compliance** — Legal adherence by renters to lease covenants and local nuisance laws.
- **Property Management Compliance** — Administrative compliance ensuring brokerages handle security deposits in trust accounts legally.
