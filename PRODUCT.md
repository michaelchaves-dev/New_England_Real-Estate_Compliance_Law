# Product split — free renters / paid operators

Decision recorded September 13, 2026.

## Free (public, all six New England states)

- State rights reports in `states/`
- Comparison snapshot and renter-help directory in `resources/`
- Official handbook and legal-aid links
- Plain-language checklists

No account required. No paywall on tenant-rights text.

## Paid (commercial operators)

Designed for property-management companies, landlords, brokerages, and lawyers:

- Jurisdiction packs: notice templates mapped to statute, deposit-ledger checklists, fee-ban audits
- Change-log / watch-list monitoring when session laws land
- Portfolio gap analysis (city overlay + subsidy + lead + registration)
- Counsel-ready exhibit binders (still not a substitute for a barred attorney)

Pricing and access control live outside this public repo.

## Hard line

This repository is **not legal advice**. The free layer educates renters and reduces preventable illegal lockouts and deposit theft. The paid layer does not sell how to evade tenant protections.
