# New England renter help desk (free layer)

Informational directory only. Programs open and close. Confirm eligibility on the official site. This folder is the free public layer: rights education and crisis navigation.

**Emergency first:** if you are in immediate danger, call 911. If you are being locked out or utilities were shut off to force you out, call local police and a legal-aid line the same day.

## All six states

- Fair housing: HUD FHEO, 1-800-669-9777
- Pre-1978 lead pamphlet: EPA Protect Your Family From Lead
- Disaster housing: DisasterAssistance.gov
- Dial 211 in every New England state
- Domestic violence: 1-800-799-7233
- Crisis: 988

## Utilities

Call the utility hardship line the same day a disconnect notice arrives. If the landlord shut off service to force a move-out, treat it as a possible illegal eviction.

## Renters insurance

Typical HO-4 covers belongings, liability, and extra living expense after a covered fire. It does not replace the landlord building policy and does not excuse a habitability violation.
