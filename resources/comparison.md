# Six-state snapshot (residential)

Figures are the **statewide default** unless a city note says otherwise. Always read the state file. Not legal advice. Updated **September 13, 2026**.

| Topic | MA | NH | ME | VT | CT | RI |
|-------|----|----|----|----|----|----|
| Core statute | G.L. c. 186 / 239 | RSA 540 / 540-A | 14 M.R.S. 6001+ / 710-A | 9 V.S.A. ch. 137 | C.G.S. Title 47a | R.I.G.L. 34-18 |
| Security deposit cap | 1 month | 1 month or $100, whichever greater | 2 months (Portland covered: 1 month) | No statewide cap (Burlington/Barre local) | 2 months; 1 month if tenant 62+ | 1 month |
| Deposit interest | Yes (c.186 s.15B) | Yes in defined cases | No statewide | No statewide | Yes (Banking Commissioner rate) | No statewide |
| Separate account | MA bank, interest-bearing | Yes | Yes, creditor-protected | Not the VT headline rule | CT escrow required | Not required |
| Return deadline | 30 days + statutory receipts | 30 days | 21 days at-will; max 30 if lease | 14 days | Later of 21 days or 15 after forwarding address | 20 days |
| Wrongful-withhold penalty | Up to 3x + fees | Double / enhanced | Double + fees after 7-day notice | Forfeit + possible double | 2x deposit | 2x amount withheld + fees |
| Application fee | Limited; broker-fee shift (2025) | Check RSA / local | Banned (actual screening cost only) | Banned | Banned (screening report cap ~$50+CPI) | No VT-style statewide ban in 34-18 |
| Rent-increase notice | Lease / 30-day M2M; cities may add | 30-day typical; 60-day nonrenewal on 12-month leases (2026) | No statewide % cap; Portland AIP + 90 days | 60 days written (Burlington often 90) | Lease / notice-to-quit overlay | 60 days; 120 days M2M over age 62 |
| Statewide rent cap | No (some cities have) | No | No (Portland / S. Portland yes) | No | No | No |
| Nonpayment first paper | 14-day notice to quit | 7-day demand typical | 7-day often | 14-day | 3-day notice to quit after grace | 15 days late then 5-day pay-or-quit |
| Entry notice | Reasonable / lease | RSA 540-A | 24 hours presumed reasonable | 48 hours; 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. | Reasonable (no fixed hours) | 2 days |
| Self-help eviction | Illegal | RSA 540-A prohibited | Illegal | Illegal | Illegal | Illegal; up to 3x rent |
