# Maine Tenant Rights Report

**Scope:** Current as of September 13, 2026. Primary statutes: 14 M.R.S. ss. 6001 et seq. and Chapter 710-A. Consumer guidance: Maine Attorney General tenant-rights pages.

## Executive Summary

No application fees statewide (actual cost of one screening report allowed with a full copy); security deposit capped at two months rent; deposit in a separate creditor-protected account; return in 21 days (tenancy at will) or up to 30 days if a written lease so provides; double damages after a 7-day pre-suit notice; implied warranty of habitability; forcible entry and detainer after a notice to quit. Portland adds rent control, a one-month local deposit cap, and longer termination notice.

## 1. High Severity

### Application fees

Maine AG: no application fees. Pass-through of the actual cost of one screening process only if the applicant receives a complete copy. Written total-price disclosure of costs if accepted.

### Security deposits (ch. 710-A)

Cap: not more than two months rent for the unit (s. 6032). Separate bank account beyond landlord creditors; tenant may demand institution and account number. Account violation remedy: actual damages, $500, or one month rent, whichever is greatest (s. 6038). Return or itemized statement: 21 days at will; lease may set up to 30 days (s. 6033). Miss the deadline: forfeit the right to withhold any part. Wrongful retention: double the portion withheld plus fees; 7-day pre-suit notice; landlord bears the burden (s. 6034). Portland covered units: local one-month cap and application-fee ban.

### Rent increases

No statewide percentage cap. Portland: Allowable Increase Percentage 2.2% calendar 2026 (2.6% announced for 2027), 90-day written notice, one increase per 12 months. South Portland: 10% annual cap on buildings of 16+ commonly owned units.

## 2. Habitability

Implied warranty of fitness for human habitation. Repair-and-deduct and court escrow exist after written notice; commonly 14 days and up to one month rent for repair-and-deduct. Confirm heat target in the municipal code (often 68F when landlord-supplied).

## 3. Entry

s. 6025: reasonable notice; 24 hours presumed reasonable.

## 4. Eviction

Tenancy at will no-cause: often 30-day notice to quit. For cause / nonpayment: often 7-day notice. Writ of possession generally 7 days after judgment. Portland overlay: generally 90 days to terminate except 7-day for-cause; shorter notices require paying one or two months rent as inconvenience compensation.

## 5-7. Lockouts, retaliation, fair housing

Self-help prohibited. Anti-retaliation after condition complaints. Maine Human Rights Act plus federal FHA. Lead rules on pre-1978 housing.

## 8. Local overlay

Portland Rent Board and mandatory Housing Rights document. Confirm 2026 ch. 767 tenant-remedy text on the Revisor site.

## Checklist

1. No application fee; one actual-cost screening with a copy.
2. Deposit at most 2 months statewide (1 month in Portland covered units); separate account; 21/30-day return.
3. 24-hour presumed entry notice.
4. Match notice-to-quit length; never lock out.
5. If Portland, run the Rent Board checklist before any increase or termination.
