# Connecticut Tenant Rights Report

**Scope:** Current as of September 13, 2026. Primary statutes: Conn. Gen. Stat. Title 47a. Deposit oversight: Department of Banking.

## Executive Summary

Two-month security-deposit cap (one month if the tenant is 62 or older); mandatory interest in a Connecticut escrow account; 21-day return clock; ban on application / move-in / move-out fees with a capped screening-report exception; summary-process eviction after a notice to quit; reasonable heat often applied as 65F when the landlord supplies heat.

## 1. High Severity

### Fees (s. 47a-4d / P.A. 23-207)

No payment for processing, reviewing, or accepting an application, and no move-in or move-out fee. Allowed: security deposit, first month rent, key or special-equipment deposit, and a tenant screening report fee capped at $50 plus annual CPI. Tenant gets a copy of the report and a receipt.

### Security deposits (s. 47a-21)

Cap: two months if under 62; one month if 62+. Excess over one month must be returned on request after the tenant turns 62. Escrow in a Connecticut institution; written notice of bank within 30 days. Interest at the Banking Commissioner rate. Return: later of 21 days after termination or 15 days after written forwarding address. Miss (other than interest-only): twice the deposit paid.

Preoccupancy walk-through: landlord may not charge for conditions noted on that walk-through.

## 2. Habitability

s. 47a-7: codes, repairs, common areas, water, hot water, reasonable heat. Tenant remedies for essential-service failure after notice include procuring substitute heat and deducting reasonable cost and substitute housing after 48 hours (s. 47a-13).

## 3. Entry

s. 47a-16: reasonable written or oral notice; reasonable times; no harassment. No fixed 24-hour statute.

## 4. Eviction

Notice to quit is not a lockout. Nonpayment: grace of 9 days (4 days week-to-week) then at least a 3-day notice to quit. Material lease violation: often a 15-day notice to remedy (s. 47a-15) before termination.

## 5-7. Illegal lockouts, retaliation, fair housing

Self-help is illegal. Retaliation presumption after good-faith complaints (ss. 47a-20 / 47a-33). Source-of-income discrimination is a high-severity CT risk. Lead disclosure on pre-1978 housing.

## 8. Local overlay

City rental registration and housing codes (Hartford, New Haven, Bridgeport, Stamford, and others). Check the city before a no-cause notice.

## Checklist

1. No app / move-in / move-out fees; screening fee inside the cap.
2. Deposit cap by age; CT escrow; interest; 21/15-day return.
3. Walk-through; do not charge for pre-existing conditions.
4. Heat when landlord-supplied; written repair log.
5. Court process only.
